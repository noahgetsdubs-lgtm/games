The game I made is Breakout; it is essentially a 1:1 recreation of the Atari game. 

The controls are very simple: left arrow to move the paddle left, and right arrow to move the paddle right.

Powerups:

I added 3 powerups to the game that have a 1 in 5 chance of spawning when a brick is broken.

1. Green - This is the multiplier powerup. If you collect it, every ball gets turned into three balls. Each one goes off in its own direction and can break the bricks. If these balls hit the bottom of the map, they will be removed, but the game won't end unless it is the last ball
2. Blue - This power-up makes the paddle bigger for the rest of the game.
3. Red - This power-up makes the paddle's movement travel a larger distance for every frame.
